Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

1.Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information ProCoach OS collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with, across both products:

  • Individualthe on-device coaching app, used by a single coach.
  • Academythe cloud platform, used by multiple coaches within an organisation.

These two products handle data differently in some important ways, so this policy is specific about which parts apply to which product.

2.The most important distinction: where your data actually lives

IndividualOn-device by design. Your players, sessions, assessments, and match records are stored directly on your own device (in your browser's local storage), not on our servers. We do not have a copy of this data and cannot see, recover, or back it up for you — that responsibility is yours, using the app's own export/backup feature.

AcademyStored in the cloud. Because multiple coaches need to see the same data, Academy organisations' players, sessions, assessments, teams, and membership records are stored in our cloud database (provided by Supabase), not on any single device.

3.What we collect

Account and billing information (both products)

  • Your email address, used for trial signup, login verification codes, and account-related communication.
  • Subscription and billing status (plan tier, trial dates, renewal dates). Payment card details are handled entirely by our payment processors (Dodo Payments for the Individual plan, Razorpay for Academy) — we do not receive or store your full card number.

IndividualCoaching data

Player names, session plans, drills, assessments, match records, and reflections you enter are stored only on your device, as described above. We never receive this content.

AcademyCoaching and organisation data

The same categories of coaching data, plus organisation structure — coach names, roles (owner, admin, head coach, assistant coach), and team membership — stored in our cloud database so your coaching staff can share a workspace.

Technical information (both products)

Basic technical data such as browser type and general usage patterns may be collected to help us understand how the Service is used and to diagnose issues. This is not linked to your coaching data.

4.Data about players, including minors

ProCoach OS is used by coaches to record information about the players they coach, who are very often minors. In this context, the coach or academy is the one entering and controlling this data — not the player themselves creating an account. If you are a coach or academy using this Service:

  • You are responsible for having the appropriate authority or parental/guardian consent, as required by the law in your area, to record information about the players you coach — particularly minors.
  • You should only enter information about a player that is reasonably necessary for coaching purposes (performance data, attendance, development notes).

We do not knowingly allow minors to create their own accounts or interact directly with the Service — access is through the coach or academy's own account.

6.How we use data

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including verifying your license or subscription status.
  • To communicate with you about your account, trial, or billing.
  • AcademyTo operate the shared cloud workspace, including enforcing role-based access within your organisation.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the Service over time.

7.Data retention and deletion

IndividualBecause your coaching data lives on your own device, it stays until you delete it yourself (through the app or by clearing your browser's storage) — we have no ability to delete it for you, since we never had a copy.

AcademyCoaching and organisation data is retained for as long as your organisation's account is active. If you close your account, you can request deletion of your organisation's data by contacting us at the address below.

Account and billing records (like email address and subscription history) may be retained for a reasonable period after account closure, where necessary for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8.Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. For the Individual app, the built-in backup/export feature already gives you a full copy of your data at any time. For anything else, or for Academy data, contact us using the details below and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

9.Security

We use reasonable technical measures — including encrypted connections and access controls — to protect the data we do hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10.Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses browser local storage and similar technologies as a core part of how it functions — for example, to keep you signed in and to store Individual-app coaching data on your device. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

11.International users

ProCoach OS is operated from India, and data processed through our cloud services (Academy) may be stored or processed outside your home country. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide reasonable notice, such as an in-app notification or an email to your account address.

13.Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to hello@procoachos.in.
